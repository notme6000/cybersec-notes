# 1. LAN (Local Area Network)

### Definition

A **Local Area Network (LAN)** is a network that connects computers and devices within a **small geographic area** such as a home, school, or office.

### Characteristics

* Covers a small area (room, building, campus)
* High data transfer speed (100 Mbps to several Gbps)
* Low latency and high reliability
* Usually privately owned and managed

### Technologies Used

* Ethernet (wired)
* Wi-Fi (wireless LAN)

### Devices Used

* Switch
* Router
* Access Point
* Computers, printers, servers

### Examples

* Home Wi-Fi network
* Office network
* College lab network

---

# 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

### Definition

A **Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)** connects multiple LANs across a **city or metropolitan region**.

### Characteristics

* Covers a larger area than LAN (city-level)
* Moderate to high speed
* Can be public or privately managed
* Uses high-capacity backbone infrastructure

### Technologies Used

* Fiber optic cables
* Metro Ethernet
* Wireless backhaul

### Examples

* City-wide internet service networks
* Cable TV networks in a city
* Government/municipality networks

---

# 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)

### Definition

A **Wide Area Network (WAN)** connects multiple LANs and MANs over **large geographic areas**, such as countries or continents.

### Characteristics

* Covers very large areas (national/global)
* Lower speed compared to LAN (due to distance)
* Higher latency
* Often owned by multiple organizations

### Technologies Used

* Leased lines
* Satellite communication
* MPLS (Multiprotocol Label Switching)
* Internet backbone infrastructure

### Examples

* The Internet
* Banking networks across countries
* Corporate networks connecting multiple branches

---

# Key Differences

| Feature   | LAN              | MAN            | WAN                    |
| --------- | ---------------- | -------------- | ---------------------- |
| Coverage  | Small (building) | City           | Country/Global         |
| Speed     | Very high        | High           | Moderate               |
| Ownership | Private          | Public/Private | Multiple organizations |
| Cost      | Low              | Medium         | High                   |
| Latency   | Low              | Medium         | High                   |

---

# Simple Way to Remember

* **LAN** → Small (Home/Office)
* **MAN** → Medium (City)
* **WAN** → Large (World)
